About Me

I am Feng Yu, a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at the National University of Singapore (NUS). I am fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Bingsheng He and Prof. Weng-fai Wong. My research focuses on high-performance computing and architecture design for next-generation accelerators. Prior to my doctoral studies, I obtained both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees from Southeast University, China.
